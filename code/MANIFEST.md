Paths are relative to this public repository. This is a producer/output map, not a new execution of every campaign.
CSV/JSON and numerical text outputs are historical generated results. Expensive runs are not launched by packaging.
Raw downloaded data, trajectory arrays and large per-record caches are excluded; use the fetchers and registered protocols.
Only the phase-one simulated world and Q4 replay needed by §9 and Online Resource 1 are included.
Superseded runs are distinguished explicitly where the paper reports them.
