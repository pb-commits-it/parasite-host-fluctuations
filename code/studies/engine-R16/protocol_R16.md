# R16 protocol, written before computation

All values are illustrative. This is a numerical factorial study, not an empirical fit or an exact finite-noise boundary proof. Only local files, Python, numpy and scipy are used. Every Python invocation uses `python3 -I`. The report and tables will be emitted by the run script.

## Design and admissibility

The requested cycles are long adult stage (1,24), sigma 0.6 or 1.4, memory 0.4; moderate (1,7.5), sigma 1.4, memory 0.75; short (1,2.3), sigma 0.7 or 1.2, memory 0.3. Each has all 32 settings of E,S,A,P,M at coarse and four-times-finer steps. Bit order is ESAPM. Zero is off. The coarse exposed stage is a fixed delay of one step. The fine exposed stage is memoryless with mean four fine steps. All other mean stays multiply by four. Fine host memory is the fourth root of coarse memory. Fine long-stage death is 1-0.99**0.25. Fine movement is 1-0.85**0.25, matching retention over a coarse step. Movement occurs after demography, including arrivals and residents in every sub-stage of the named mobile stage. Island destinations include the departure patch. Transit loss is zero in this design.

E off uses a host-dependent yield and fixed leaving. E on uses encounter-dependent leaving and death equal to half the total mean exit probability, with the same mean matrix after birth calibration. A on uses two labelled, equal-share entrances to the same long-stage compartment; its encounter probability is the sum of the two share-weighted host probabilities. S on uses four geometric sub-stages, each with one quarter of the specified long-stage mean. M is independent survival at each step before normal progression. Every return to the exposed stage is a birth. Births are calibrated using the next-generation matrix so that the mean matrix is critical. The unraised-birth death cost is reported separately as minus the change in log R0.

Sigma means log standard deviation of the matched lognormal host. The bounded host has variance exp(sigma**2)-1 and mean one. Its low level is 0.05. Its high level is 1+variance/0.95. The high-state probability is 0.95**2/(variance+0.95**2). Each host independently refreshes from this law with probability 1-phi per step. E off and on use exactly this same host law. The second-order value expands this fixed standardized two-state law about zero amplitude, with first derivative sigma; it is not a substitution of raw variance into a lognormal formula.

Impossible cells remain in the full table as inadmissible. Contact requires positive mean staying and positive staying at every host level. Four sub-stages require mean at least four steps. In particular coarse E on is impossible, coarse short S on is impossible, and sigma 1.4 contact is impossible even at the requested fine step: its variance exceeds the maximum allowed by the contact probability bounds. No parameter is silently changed. No factorial effect requiring missing cells is called a full factorial effect. Available balanced subdesign contrasts are labelled conditional. This is a limitation of the requested design, not evidence against a biological conclusion.

## Measurements

The engine compiles labelled edges. It computes mean growth, next-generation R0, reference transition probabilities, labelled edge frequencies, exact edge Jensen bound J, its quadratic version J2, the general resolvent coefficient C and threshold coefficient D2=C/b, with b the birth-edge frequency. Bounds and D are per birth generation. V is measured from first-order rewards on independent reference paths in a shared environment, divided by the same b. The identity uses J2, not the finite-amplitude J. Periodic mean matrices have no resolvent coefficient returned; products and paths start in a single cohort. Route branching can make an otherwise deterministic-timing network aperiodic, so graph period is checked, not guessed from stage names.

D is a root of actual matrix-product growth under a common birth tilt. Independent antithetic pairs are the sampling units. All root evaluations reuse the same forcing. Pairwise growth residuals divided by the measured birth slope estimate D's sampling standard error. A convergence residual is printed. Finite-horizon bias is not included in this error. Root tolerance is 1e-7 in birth-log units; a failed root is reported, not replaced by D2.

Initial budget: 8 antithetic pairs, 12000 retained coarse steps or 48000 fine steps, with burn-in at least 1000 coarse steps or ten mean generations. V uses 16 independent environments, 96 paths each, and two consecutive windows of at least 500 coarse steps or ten generations. Window differencing removes the leading endpoint contribution. These small replicate counts preserve the whole requested design within the time budget. The program uses three worker processes with one BLAS thread each. It records wall time. If a pilot projects more than 35 minutes, reduce replicate counts before reducing any design dimension, record the change, and keep a hard 40-minute limit.

Moderate-cycle extinction is measured at 2000 reference mean generations, from 10000 individuals in the exposed stage (divided across patches), with log R0=D+0.02. There are 128 independent environments and independent endpoint Bernoulli trials. Conditional extinction probabilities are computed by backward composition of the exact Poisson offspring generating functions. This samples extinction indicators with exactly the branching-process endpoint law and avoids integer overflow in surviving populations. It is not a simulation of every population trajectory. Sampling uses independent environments, not antithetic pairs, for the binomial standard error. The initial cohort, horizon in steps and trials are printed. Environmental D uncertainty is reported separately, not included in the binomial error.

## Decisions fixed in advance

Cost fails if D+4SE<0. All negative point estimates are also listed. Parking fails in a comparison if the longer-stage loss exceeds the shorter by more than four combined standard errors. Shape compares S on against off separately in every available background. Cross-cycle comparison uses the matched sigma 1.4 pair (long versus moderate); host memory also differs, so it cannot isolate duration. No unmatched-sigma comparison is presented as a duration experiment. Additional matched-duration/memory interventions are not in this design.

Routes are assessed by the A contrast in each available background. A reliably negative contrast supports alternative-route protection; it does not refute the stated equivalence of sequential hosts and sub-stages. An adverse contrast would refute universal protection by alternatives. Smallest failures mean the fewest on switches among the actually admissible cells; ties are retained. The grid does not locate exact continuous boundaries or establish global minimality.

The bound fails numerically if D-J>4SE+1e-7. Contact uses the staying-edge bound. The transfer-only bound is reported separately to expose its scope. Second order has a wrong sign when D2 and D have opposite signs; resolved wrong signs additionally require |D|>4SE. A size discrepancy is |D2-D|>0.30|D|; resolved discrepancies also exceed 4SE. Main effects are mean(on)-mean(off). Interactions are mean(11)-mean(10)-mean(01)+mean(00). Independent cell variances propagate with squared contrast weights. Interactions are listed if their magnitude exceeds four standard errors. Every background is enumerated for parking and A comparisons, with unavailable cells explicit. Extinction ordering is compared for all moderate pairs; a resolved reversal requires both a four-SE D separation and a four-binomial-SE extinction separation in the opposite direction. Rank correlation is descriptive.

## Tests before interpretation

The engine tests lock relative algebra tolerance at 1e-9 for at least 50 random cases per formula family. Two small-noise product checks per family use four standard errors plus 5 percent of the prediction. Five independently measured lineage checks use four standard errors plus 1 percent of J2. Every product check also tests D<=J with four standard errors plus 1e-7. Constant-environment Poisson extinction is compared with the independently iterated generating-function fixed point within four binomial standard errors. Harness failures retain numbered attempt outputs. A formula failure stops interpretation and is reported without changing formulas or tolerances.

## Not done

No proof of finite-noise boundaries, empirical fitting, host feedback, continuous time, or rigorous finite-horizon error bound. Inadmissible cells cannot establish five-switch factorial effects. The bounded law matches mean and variance, not all moments of the lognormal host. Forward trajectories in explosive whole-number populations are not required for the exact endpoint extinction sampler.
