# R18 protocol — frozen before outcome computation

Only files in this directory will be read or written. No network. Python is invoked with `python3 -I`. The final run uses at most three worker processes and a 2,350 second wall-clock budget, leaving 50 seconds for shutdown. Code preparation and an explicitly labelled engineering attempt are separate. No outcome-dependent change of a comparison or tolerance is allowed.

## Model

Time is discrete. F counts individual fluctuating-stage parasites. C_b counts occupied steady hosts carrying b parasites. Every packet founds a new host. Hosts do not merge. Empty hosts disappear. Initially F=0 and the steady stage contains exactly 10,000 parasites, either 10,000 hosts of burden 1 or 100 hosts of burden 100.

The fluctuating mean stay is 10; the steady mean stay is L in {10,40,160}. Put q=1/L and s=1-q. The shared-loss fraction a is 0, 0.5, or 1. Host survival per step is s^a and individual survival conditional on a living host is s^(1-a). These are independent clocks; their product is s. Thus a is a share of the log hazard, not of the discrete death probability. All parasites of a dying host die together.

To embed the supplied independent law exactly, shedding is a burst process. Each parasite has a Bernoulli(q) shedding mark per step. A mark releases Poisson(g_S/q) F offspring before the loss clocks act. Individual deaths are a subset of marks: mark-conditioned death probability [1-s^(1-a)]/q. Host death is independent and follows shedding. Equivalently first sample individual deaths, mark each remaining parasite with probability [q-(1-s^(1-a))]/s^(1-a), shed from all marks, then kill hosts. The shedding marginal is the same for every a. At a=0 shedding is terminal; at a=1 it occurs during the host's life. This specified correlation with individual loss is part of the law, not a universal biological assumption.

Each F independently leaves with probability 0.1. Conditional on the number leaving, successful packets are Poisson with mean leavers*g_F*H/(0.1*E[K]). Fixed K is 1, 10, or 100. The clumped supplement has P(K=1)=P(K=19)=1/2, E[K]=10. Each arriving packet founds one host. This is a branching law with unlimited susceptible steady hosts. It excludes competition, capacity, reinfection of occupied hosts, and spatial correlation between host deaths.

The conditional parasite mean matrix, in order F,S, is [[0.9,g_S],[g_F*H,1-1/L]]. It is independent of a, packet law, and initial grouping. At a=0,K=1 this is the supplied binomial-stayer/Poisson-offspring ring.

Environment A: Z is stationary Gaussian AR(1), memory .9; H=exp(.15 Z-.15^2/2). Environment B: stationary symmetric on/off Markov chain; each state switches with probability 1/G. H=2 on and 0 off. Both states have mean duration G; the stationary mean transmission multiplier is exactly one. G is 5,20,60,200. Environment is shared by all hosts within a replicate and independent between replicates.

## Matching and calibration

Main comparisons set g_F=0.1+r and g_S=1/L+r. Both columns of the mean matrix at H=1 sum to 1+r. Its left Perron vector is exactly (1,1), in common parasite units. Every initial condition therefore has exactly 10,000 reproductive-value units, for every L and law.

What is matched instead of ln R0-D: the simulated long-run log growth of the continuous-quantity matrix product, times the nominal generation length Tgen=L+10, is m=0.005. R0=g_F*g_S/(0.1/L). Define D=Tgen*[log(1+r)-lambda_random], the measured environmental loss per nominal generation. Away from the threshold Tgen*log(1+r) is not exactly ln R0, so the report must not label this ln R0-D matching. r is calibrated separately for each environment and L, then held fixed across all demographic laws. Report r,R0,D, calibration uncertainty, and an independent check of m. Use normalized matrix products, stationary environments, 32 paths of 40,000 steps after 2,000 burn-in; a common-path vectorized bracket/bisection calibrates all L together. Validation uses an independent seed and the same length. Matching tolerance: |m_observed-.005| <= max(.002,4 estimated standard errors). If sampling uncertainty is larger than the margin, disclose this; do not claim the growth sign is resolved.

No grouped 'none' comparator is included. Removing S removes both its residence and its hosts, so no canonical comparator preserves these laws. A one-stage ring appears only in the historical validation, where all parasites are independent.

## Design, budget, and endpoints

Primary full factorial: a={0,.5,1} x K={1,10,100} x L={10,40,160} x environment={A,B20,B200} x initial burden={1,100}: 162 cells. Main horizon 1,700 calendar steps. Also read each path at 10 nominal generations: 200,500,1,700 steps. These are finite horizons, much shorter than the historical experiment; no eventual-extinction inference is allowed.

Each cell starts with 1,000 independent replicates. If the extinct fraction is below .02 or above .98, run 2,000 additional independent replicates and pool all 3,000. This rule is fixed, not a significance stopping rule. If performance requires reducing the factorial, first drop the clumped and additional-duration supplements, then K=10 from the primary factorial, then a=.5. Never reduce below 1,000 replicates for a retained cell. Stop scheduling before the deadline, preserve unfinished-cell status, and disclose any unrun cells. Do not silently count unfinished replicates as survivors.

Supplements, after primary cells: clumped mean-10 packets, a=0 or 1, initial burden 100, every L and main environment (18 cells); B5 and B60, a=1,K=1 or 100, initial burden 100, every L (12 cells). The order of cells is deterministic and balanced by environment and L, with complete triples scheduled together when feasible.

Record extinct-by-calendar and extinct-by-generations fractions with binomial SE; Wilson intervals (including nonzero upper bounds at 0 events); extinction-time quartiles conditional on extinction; and every replicate's minimum number of occupied steady hosts, counted at every integer step including the start. Summarize the latter among calendar survivors. A zero steady-host minimum can occur while F is still alive. Retain raw extinction times and host minima in local compressed arrays. No successful-population cutoff is permitted. Integer histogram counts represent hosts exactly; if counts exceed the safe integer range, fail the cell instead of censoring. Numerical approximations, if needed, require a prospective protocol amendment and validation.

Protection is a positive difference p_ext(short)-p_ext(long), resolved at four combined independent binomial SE, and at least .01 for a substantively visible effect. A significant negative difference is a reversal. Differences unresolved at four SE are not evidence of equality. Report both adjacent comparisons and 10 against 160. No universal monotonicity claim follows from a finite factorial. Compare rankings against D; laws with equal D but different extinction refute D as a sufficient predictor. Explore occupied-host minima and independent retained-host counts as descriptive predictors; survivor-conditioned minima cannot establish causality.

## Single-gap experiment and proof target

There is no renewal during an off period. For the retention endpoint count only initially stored parasites still in S at reopening. This differs from full-life-cycle extinction because F can remain alive. For initial host burdens b_j, host survival h(G), and independent parasite survival p(G), the exact survival probability is 1-product_j[1-h(G)*(1-(1-p(G))^b_j)]. Prove using host independence and complementation. Exponential clocks give h=exp(-a*G/tau), p=exp(-(1-a)*G/tau). The discrete main model instead has h=(1-1/L)^(aG), p=(1-1/L)^((1-a)G). At a=0 the formula reduces to 1-(1-p)^N; at a=1 it reduces to 1-(1-h)^M.

For less dispersed lifetimes use four independent exponential phases. A fresh host of mean tau has h=exp(-4G/tau)*sum_{j=0}^3(4G/tau)^j/j!. A stationary-age host has h=(1/4)*sum_{j=0}^3 P(Poisson(4G/tau)<=j). Derive both in the report. Compare shapes at the same mean host lifetime, pure shared fate, and separately independent parasites. This is a retention experiment, not a claim that the two-state main matrix remains unchanged with lifetime shape. Test fresh and stationary cohorts; report the G/tau ratios for survival .95,.5,.05, for 10,000 independent parasites and 100 hosts of burden100. Thus 'stops protecting' means absolute retention <=.05, not that the longer lifetime reverses its monotone advantage at a fixed shape. Compare short and long stages at G={5,20,60,200} and at longer gaps to locate that boundary.

Validate the formula with 20,000 independently sampled cohorts per condition, using actual exponential/gamma/geometric lifetime draws (or exact binomial numbers of independent surviving units); at mixed a sample host survival followed by individual parasite thinning. Use ratios G/tau={.5,2,5,10}, a={0,.5,1}, initial burdens 1 and100, and fresh/stationary four-phase pure shared cases. Tolerance four SE, with SE from the exact probability and a 1/n floor at boundaries.

### Prospective implementation amendment, before any numerical run

The histogram representation must also support rare, very large populations under compensated outages. Use exact numpy binomial, Poisson, and multinomial draws below 1e9. Above 1e9 use rounded Gaussian draws with the corresponding exact means and covariance, returning to exact draws whenever counts fall below the threshold. For a multinomial, use independent category normals and subtract the probability vector times their sum, which gives covariance n[diag(p)-pp^T]; enforce nonnegative counts and conserve the parent count, logging repairs. Floating point carries large integer-valued counts; beyond 2^53 it cannot resolve individual units. This is a demographic approximation, not a cutoff or a declaration of survival. Validate mean and covariance at an artificially low switch of1e3, and compare exact versus switched whole-path extinction for three constant-environment laws. Print every cell's number of approximated runs. If any repair changes more than 1e-6 of its parent population, fail. No population is removed for being large. This amendment supersedes the safe-integer failure clause above; all other tests and comparisons remain fixed.

### Engineering correction after the first attempt

The first attempt failed before any model trajectory. At the deliberately low Gaussian switch, rounding one unit per multinomial category can exceed 1e-6 of the parent count. The repair guard now allows at most one rounding unit per category OR 1e-6 of the parent count, whichever is larger. The substantive clipping tolerance is unchanged. This changes a numerical guard, not a measured comparison. The original failed output is preserved.

### Corrected factorial law, before the final run

A model review found that the reference-compatible shedding marks introduced a changing shedding/death covariance as a varied, even for singleton hosts. That undermines the intended factorial. The second attempt was stopped during the first AR cells, before any outage factorial result. Its output is retained as r18_output_attempt2.txt. None of those factorial estimates will enter the final report.

The FINAL model replaces the mark construction by independent Poisson shedding: conditional on the current S total, F offspring are Poisson(g_S*S), independently of the mortality clocks. Shedding precedes mortality; newly shed stages are separate propagules. All other transitions and matching definitions remain unchanged. Thus a has no effect on the law when all hosts carry one parasite and all packets have size1. It changes dependence only when burdens exceed1. The old mark construction above is superseded, not an alternative fitted to the outcomes.

The historical reproduction check is now explicitly a separate reference-law regression test. That law releases Poisson offspring on removal; continuous shedding cannot have the identical offspring generating function. Reproducing its three numbers validates the reference sampler, not the new model's extinction law. This distinction must appear in the report's validation and Not done sections. The three new-law PGF tests are (a,K)=(0,1),(1,10),(1,100). Their steady-host generating function is exp(K*g_S*(f-1))*[(1-s_H)+s_H*z] under pure shared fate; for an independent singleton it is exp(g_S*(f-1))*[q+(1-q)*z]. The F generating function is unchanged. The numerical mean checks use the new shedding variance g_S*S.

The stopped calibration also showed unnecessary Monte Carlo uncertainty. Final calibration uses 64 independent PAIRS of antithetic stationary environmental histories, each80,000 steps plus2,000 burn-in, and19 bisections. AR pairs use Z and -Z; outage pairs exchange on and off. Each member has the prescribed stationary law. Pair averages are the independent observations used for SE. Validation uses a new seed. The target and tolerance remain unchanged. No demographic outcomes enter calibration.

### Exact histogram optimization during the final campaign

The final campaign was briefly paused after six completed cells to replace a slow multinomial implementation. For a binomial burden transition, pool the extreme lower tail (probability at most1e-10) as ONE category. Draw the pooled multinomial. If a draw enters that category, expand it with the conditional multinomial over every original tail burden. This is the same law: multiplying the tail probability by the conditional category probabilities restores each original category probability. No tail is discarded. This reduces the number of common multinomial categories. The Gaussian large-count approximation remains as specified. Repeat the mean and PGF checks before resuming. Retain completed final-law cells and their seeds; unfinished cells are rerun from their seeds. The 40-minute budget is measured from the original final-campaign start, including the pause. The initial segment is retained as r18_output_segment1.txt. This is a computational optimization, not a new model or sampling design.

The same optimization pause was extended to use direct individual-host draws when at most3,000,000 hosts of burden greater than1 are present. The six completed dense-sampler cells retain that algorithm on a fresh rerun, so their fixed-seed results remain reproducible. All timing is still charged to the same final campaign.
