# Linked hosts: protocol written before computation

Scope: local files only; no network or literature search. All parameter values below are illustrative. Baseline in every comparison: every rate fixed at its arithmetic mean. Transfers are linear in relative host abundance, so this also equals the same system at the same mean host abundance. No fluctuating stays, host feedback from parasites, or curved encounter rates.

## Adopted model and departures

Adopt the stationary Gaussian VAR(1), the predator–prey rotation (radius 0.9, angle pi/10), shared driver (memories 0.25 and 0.85, innovation correlation 0.9), and competitors (diagonal 0.55, off-diagonal -0.35) in `other/collaborator-protocol-linked-hosts.md`. Also use its amended fast competitors (diagonal 0.02, off-diagonal -0.02, innovation correlation -0.8). Solve the discrete Lyapunov equation and standardize each log variance to one. Independent controls retain the **entire marginal covariance function**, not just lag-one correlation; for quasi-cycles these marginals are not scalar AR(1) processes.

Main ring: stage mean stays `(5, a, 5)`, `a in {1,5,50}`, transfers out of stages 0 and 1 driven by hosts 1 and 2, or swapped. The transfer out of stage 2 is steady. All staying rates are fixed. Thus the directed time from transfer 0 to transfer 1 has mean a, and the return time is a sum of two geometric stays with total mean 10. This replaces the collaborator's two-stage ring: it did not contain the user's separate steady stage. Retain two-stage `(10,a)` rings as analytical consistency controls. For prey-to-predator transmission, host 2 is the predator and enters stage 2; a long stay in stage 2 is the predator parking test. A change in stage 0 tests a separate steady stage. These are distinct interventions.

Do not adopt the collaborator's predicted signs, purported unique cancellation, or all-noise order symmetries without proof. Result 10 is explicitly continuous time. Its sign claim will be tested, not imported into discrete time. Use three worker processes at most, Python 3 with numpy/scipy, `python3 -I mechanism_checks.py`. Target final-script wall time below 15 minutes. No sub-agents or external tools.

## Reporting and failure policy

All numbers in the note and final response must come from `mechanism_checks.py` and its saved output. Every displayed closed form gets a proof hereafter or is labelled conjecture, and an independent unexpanded computation on its applicable family. The lineage-variance identity is for interpretation only, never to generate numbers. Numerical root values are computed boundaries with displayed precision, not proofs of finite-noise equality. A strict second-order sign plus the stated Gaussian remainder gives an asymptotic conclusion, not a finite-sigma guarantee. Finite-noise significance requires 5 SE; otherwise report unresolved. No multiplicity claim from pointwise confidence intervals.

If the harness fails, keep `mechanism_checks_output_attempt1.txt` (and later attempts as needed), repair it without changing the criterion silently, and describe the failure. A false conjecture is a result. Exploratory extensions must be appended here before their outcomes are computed. The final note will state H1–H4 verdicts, baselines, exact conditions, examples, proofs, uncertainty, and a final `Not done` section. Literature statements, if any, are marked “recalled, not verified”; no names of people in the findings note.
