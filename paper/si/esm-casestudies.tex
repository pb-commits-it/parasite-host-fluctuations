\section{Registered empirical case studies}\label{num-esm-casestudies}

This section documents the preliminary tick--pathogen field checks (runs R12--R12c and R14), the verification of four additional candidate experimental and observational leads (Table~\ref{num-tab-esm-leads}), and the seven pre-registered empirical case studies summarized in Section~\ref{sec:num} (Table~\ref{num-tab-casestudies}). Every analysis followed a protocol locked before outcome values were inspected, evaluated pre-declared numerical validation gates before touching empirical rows, and retained dated corrections without rewriting earlier records.

In the preliminary Cary Institute analyses \citep{ostfeld20182019,ostfeld2026b,ladeau2025}, six 2.25-ha mixed-oak plots in Dutchess County, New York, yielded $127/128$ positive annual Jolly--Seber white-footed mouse (\textit{Peromyscus leucopus}) estimates ($\sigma = 1.030$, $95\%$ two-year block-bootstrap interval $[0.762, 1.217]$, lag-one correlation $r_1 = -0.107$ $[-0.537, 0.486]$, 1991--2015) and $191/191$ positive minimum-number-alive values ($\sigma = 0.772$ $[0.624, 0.871]$, $r_1 = -0.401$ $[-0.728, -0.004]$, 1991--2025), alongside $113/114$ nonzero nymphal \textit{Borrelia burgdorferi} prevalence plot-years (1995--2013, combining sensitivity-corrected immunofluorescence and polymerase chain reaction, PCR, assays, median $53$ tested ticks per plot-year). Log prevalence varied less than log mice on $6/6$ plots in 1995--2013 ($\mathrm{SD} \in [0.295, 0.493]$ vs.\ $[0.924, 1.219]$, P1) and on $6/6$ grids in the 2014--2022 qRT-PCR holdout ($40/54$ grid-years with at least $20$ tested nymphs, $1{,}842/2{,}017$ ticks, $\mathrm{SD} \in [0.273, 1.082]$ vs.\ $[1.022, 1.391]$, B1, unchanged on $6/6$ grids after subtracting binomial sampling variance $(1-p)/(np)$, which exceeded total variance on $2/6$ grids, B3). Within-plot slopes of $\ln\mathrm{NIP}_{t+1}$ on $\ln\text{mice}_t$ were $b_1 = 0.038$ ($\mathrm{SE} = 0.058$, $95\%$ interval $[-0.067, 0.160]$, $102/104$ pairs, P2) and $b_2 = 0.051$ ($\mathrm{SE} = 0.118$, $[-0.203, 0.237]$, $40/40$ usable pairs, $|b_2 - b_1| = 0.013 \le 0.264$, B2), both compatible with $[0, 1 - \bar{p}]$ and with zero. As corrected in a dated note, Section~\ref{sec:dyn}'s constant-host bound restricts proportional changes in larval infection pressure rather than mouse numbers alone, so P1, B1, and R14's T2 are descriptive comparisons rather than valid tests of the theory. In R12c (1995--2013), regressing log August larval tick burden per mouse (`LPM`) on log `PL\_grid` gave $\kappa_2 = -0.170$ ($\mathrm{SE} = 0.100$, $[-0.407, -0.021]$, $107/108$ pairs, holding inside $[-1, 0]$), whereas nymphal burden per mouse ($\kappa_1$) was absent ($0/59$ columns). Evaluating the two-stage cycle (stays $1$ and $10/4.4 = 2.273$ steps of $4.4\ \mathrm{mo}$, $200$ populations, $200{,}000$ steps) with mean-one multipliers $\exp(\kappa_i \sigma Z_t - \kappa_i^2 \sigma^2/2)$ across $\kappa_1 \in \{0, 0.5, 1\}$ and $\kappa_2 \in \{-0.370, -0.170, +0.030\}$ gave $D \in [0.00016, 0.20843]$ at $\sigma = 1.0304$ ($0.17351 \pm 0.00016$ at $\kappa_1 = 1, \kappa_2 = -0.170$ vs.\ $0.20181 \pm 0.00019$ at $\kappa_2 = 0$) and $D \in [0.00008, 0.10975]$ at $\sigma = 0.772$, with exact-to-quadratic ratios of $1.29$--$8.41$ in the $8/18$ opposite-sign cases with $\kappa_1 \ge 0.5$.

Across the $13$ eastern and central United States sites downloaded from the National Ecological Observatory Network \citep[RELEASE-2026 plus provisional 2020--2021 and 2025--2026 site-months, accessed 14 November 2026, $2{,}393$ site-months and $4{,}049$ files;][]{neon2026a,neon2026b,neon2026c}, $6/13$ sites (`BLAN`, `HARV`, `ORNL`, `SCBI`, `SERC`, `TREE`) met the registered cutoff of at least $100$ tested \textit{Ixodes scapularis} nymphs across at least four years ($4{,}494/4{,}591$ DNA-quality-verified nymphs). Primary cross-site Spearman correlation between site prevalence and \textit{Peromyscus} log-index SD ($\sigma_{\mathrm{site}} \in \{0.399, 0.989, 0.505, 0.564, 0.571, 0.482\}$) was $\rho = +0.4857$ (exact one-sided $p = 0.8514$ negative, $p = 0.1778$ positive across $720$ permutations; T1 not decided). Check T2 failed ($2/6$ sites, `BLAN` and `ORNL`, had $\mathrm{SD}(\ln\mathrm{NIP}) > \mathrm{SD}(\ln\text{index})$ against a $1.5$-site limit; subtracting binomial sampling variance set `ORNL` to $0$ and left `BLAN` at $0.522 > 0.247$, $1/6$), while pooled within-site lagged slope T3 held by its wide band ($b = 0.2464$, $\mathrm{SE} = 0.1495$, $46/50$ site-year pairs, inside $[0, 0.7979]$). Post hoc evaluation across $200{,}000$ simulated six-site datasets using the corrected exact support threshold $\rho \le -29/35$ ($21/720 = 2.92\%$ of untied rankings, correcting an initial $\rho \le -0.829$ coding error that kept $12/720 = 1.67\%$) showed that against between-site $\ln R_0$ variation of $\tau = 0.17$ ($0.10$ and $0.30$ bracketing), power to reach support was $6.8\%$ ($9.7\%$ and $4.9\%$) at full proportional mouse entry ($\kappa = 1$), $3.5\%$ ($3.9\%$ and $3.3\%$) at $\kappa = 0.5$, and $3.0\%$ ($3.0\%$ and $3.0\%$) at $\kappa = 0.25$, against a $2.9\%$ ($2.7\%$ and $2.9\%$) null rate.

\subsection{Museum fish parasites in Puget Sound (\texttt{wood})}\label{num-esm-wood}

\paragraph{Protocol summary.}
The protocol (locked 14 November 2026, 22:31 CST, with SHA-256 hashes recorded for the blind trait table and pre-analysis power outputs prior to accessing count outcomes; environmental amendment registered 15 November 2026, 00:24 CST) asked whether four pre-coded life-cycle traits explain century-scale temporal abundance slopes in Puget Sound marine fish parasites beyond obligate host number (`nhosts`: $1$, $2$, or $3+$). Following a mandatory Step~1 reproduction gate on the four published negative-binomial generalized linear mixed models (NB-GLMMs), Step~2 tested an omnibus maximum-likelihood (ML) precision-weighted mixed-effects meta-regression (`M7` vs.\ host-number `M3`, requiring $\Delta\mathrm{AIC}_c \le -2.0$ and parametric-bootstrap likelihood-ratio $p < 0.05$ over $B = 2{,}000$ draws) alongside restricted-ML (REML) $t$-contrasts across four traits (fluctuating obligate transfers $\beta_a < 0$, storage stage $\beta_b > 0$, alternative/paratenic host spreading $\beta_c > 0$, and active-transfer fraction $\beta_d < 0$, step-down Holm--Bonferroni adjusted) and unified one-stage Laplace NB-GLMM checks (`M7` vs.\ `M3\_tax`). Pre-analysis categorical Variance Inflation Factors had maximum $\mathrm{VIF} = 3.011$ (full rank $7/7$), with $80\%$ power at residual slope SD $0.30$ and taxonomic SD $0.15$.

\paragraph{Data provenance.}
Specimen-level counts came from \citet{wood2023} on Dryad (\url{https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.fqz612jwf}, fetched via its official Zenodo mirror \url{https://zenodo.org/records/7448240} under CC0 1.0, verified 14--15 November 2026; companion GitHub code read as plain text only) \citep{wood2022}. One observation row is one parasite-code count in one fluid-preserved fish, comprising $8{,}754$ rows across $699$ fish of eight species collected 1880--2019 (collection-year SD $30.268\ \mathrm{yr}$), $85$ parasite codes ($20/85$, $21/85$, and $44/85$ in the $1$-, $2$-, and $3+$-host groups), and $97$ host--parasite pairs (minimum prevalence $0.0467 \ge 0.04$), summing to $17{,}259$ counted parasites. Blind life-cycle traits were locked before outcome inspection from published life-cycle databases (Benesh helminth database, Global Mammal Parasite Database, World Register of Marine Species, and Global Biotic Interactions; $37/37$ local manifest hashes verified).

\paragraph{Confounders and handling.}
Specimen-level overdispersion, fish body size, preservation variation, and multi-host taxon sharing were controlled through negative-binomial dispersion parameters, fish-specimen random intercepts ($699$ fish), host--parasite pair random slopes, and $33$ higher-taxon random-slope groups (including $5/33$ unresolved bins). Larval-versus-adult stage confounding in dissected fish was evaluated in complete-case sensitivity `S2b` (`terminal\_in\_fish`, $78/85$ taxa, $8{,}086/8{,}754$ rows), and the theoretical storage-by-waiting-mortality interaction (`mech-C1h`) was tested via `S4` (`storage\_stage * prop\_active\_transfers`, $85/85$ taxa). Unmeasured densities, competence, and variances of unobserved invertebrate and vertebrate hosts, generic genus/family trait coding, mean host decline, food-web restructuring, and phenological mismatch could not be separated from these museum counts and were recorded as causal-identification limits.

\paragraph{Weather and environment.}
All $699$ fish lacked collection month and day ($0/699$ subannual dates), and $684/699$ fish carried $149$ distinct capture coordinates ($15/699$ lacked coordinates). Annual mean sea-surface temperature (`sst\_mean`) and within-year monthly SD (`sst\_monthly\_sd`) from NOAA Extended Reconstructed Sea Surface Temperature v6 (\url{https://psl.noaa.gov/thredds/ncss/grid/Datasets/noaa.ersst.v6/sst.mnmean.nc/dataset.html}, accessed 15 November 2026 via public OPeNDAP; nearest $2^\circ$ ocean cell $48^{\circ}\mathrm{N}, 124^{\circ}\mathrm{W}$ at $74.1$--$144.5\ \mathrm{km}$, median $111.4\ \mathrm{km}$) matched $684/699$ fish across $72$ sampled years (1895--2019, $8{,}632/8{,}754$ rows; standardized design condition number $1.747$, SST--year $r = 0.362$). Secondary point observations from Fisheries and Oceans Canada Race Rocks lighthouse (\url{https://www.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/science/data-donnees/lightstations-phares/index-eng.html}, $40.4$--$141.1\ \mathrm{km}$) matched $659/699$ fish across $68$ years (post hoc quality control masking one invalid $99.9\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$ July 1966 observer entry gave $0.049\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$ mean absolute disagreement across $65$ overlapping years). Entering ERSSTv6 into the NB-GLMM (`W1\_ersst`) worsened fit ($\Delta\mathrm{AIC}_c = +4.778$), gave a baseline $3+$-host thermal association of $-12.3\%\ {}^{\circ}\mathrm{C}^{-1}$ ($[-31.7\%, 12.5\%]$), and shifted `host\_3plus` and `storage\_stage` slopes by $\ge 20\%$ ($21.7\%$ and $13.2\%$ on matched $8{,}632$ rows; $17.6\%$ and $21.0\%$ vs.\ full sample), leaving the study verdict **not decided**.

\paragraph{Registered results.}
Step~1 reproduced all four published host-number models (`M3\_nhosts` best at $\mathrm{AIC}_c = 17{,}072.097$, $\Delta\mathrm{AIC}_c = 1.118$ ahead of `M2\_year`, $3.324$ ahead of `M4\_partype`, and $63.568$ ahead of `M1\_null`, with decadal trends $-1.137\%$ $[-11.119\%, 9.966\%]$ for $1$ host, $+0.171\%$ $[-9.681\%, 11.098\%]$ for $2$ hosts, and $-10.914\%$ $[-17.218\%, -4.129\%]$ for $3+$ hosts). Step~2 primary outcome was **not decided**: adding the four traits worsened meta-regression ML fit ($\Delta\mathrm{AIC}_c = +7.525$, $2\Delta\ell = 2.115$, bootstrap $p = 0.7546$) and direct count-GLMM fit ($\Delta\mathrm{AIC}_c = +4.315$ with taxonomy, $+4.313$ without). None of the $4/4$ primary REML contrasts was significant (`df` $= 78$, all Holm $p = 1.000$): fluctuating transfers $-0.061$ ($[-0.223, 0.101]$, $p = 0.455$), storage $+0.079$ ($[-0.173, 0.331]$, $p = 0.534$), spreading $+0.083$ ($[-0.188, 0.354]$, $p = 0.543$), and active fraction $+0.082$ ($[-0.250, 0.415]$, $p = 0.623$), with within-$3+$ REML contrasts ($44/85$ taxa, `df` $= 39$) likewise all Holm $p = 1.000$ and `host\_3plus` slope attenuation reaching only $26.4\%$ (meta REML) and $10.6\%$ (count GLMM). Sensitivity `S4` yielded a positive REML storage main effect ($+0.802$ $[0.254, 1.349]$, Holm $p = 0.0186$, interaction $-1.154$, $p = 0.0152$) that collapsed under ML ($\hat{\sigma}_{\mathrm{tax}}^2 = 0$, $\Delta\mathrm{AIC}_c = +9.485$) and in the count GLMM ($\Delta\mathrm{AIC}_c = +5.730$), while `S2b` (`terminal\_in\_fish`) did not survive Holm adjustment ($p_{\mathrm{Holm}} = 0.1870$, $\Delta\mathrm{AIC}_c = +4.371$ meta ML, $+1.179$ count GLMM).

\paragraph{Independent replication and corrections.}
An independent clean-room implementation using exact implicit-differentiation Laplace NB-GLMMs and profile ML/REML meta-regression reproduced every Step~1, Step~2, `W1\_ersst`, and sensitivity decision (**verdict: reproduced**). Two non-decision-changing numerical corrections were recorded on 15 November 2026: (i) removing a redundant one-level group-intercept variance parameter in the within-$3+$ count GLMM (`unified\_M8\_within\_3plus`, $44/85$ taxa) reduced parameter count from $17$ to $16$ and $\mathrm{AIC}_c$ from $11{,}113.003$ to $11{,}110.989$ (difference $2.014$, slope changes $< 3 \times 10^{-5}$, REML table unaffected); and (ii) replacing literal $1.96$ with $z_{0.975} = 1.959964$ shifted normal Wald endpoints by at most $1.1 \times 10^{-5}$ (`W1` baseline $-31.670\%$ to $12.450\%$, still $-31.7\%$ to $12.5\%$).

\paragraph{Departures.}
Before fitting trait models, an unused draft helper that exponentiated `loglogsigma\_phi` twice was replaced by a verified helper matching the published TMB C++ parameterization, initial VIF prose ($< 1.80$) was corrected to the actual categorical design ($\max\mathrm{VIF} = 3.011$), higher-taxon group counts were corrected from $27$ to $33$ (including $5/33$ unresolved bins), and constrained variance-boundary bootstrap fits were refit with projected-gradient checks on the identical random stream without altering $p = 0.7546$. All intermediate failed solver attempts were retained as `*attempt*` logs. Because museum counts neither measure stationary $R_0$ nor observe multi-host abundance variance, the non-significant trait slopes represent an unidentifiable causal design rather than a departure from proved stationary ring theorems.

\subsection{Global host abundance compilations (\texttt{lpd})}\label{num-esm-lpd}

\paragraph{Protocol summary.}
The protocol (locked 15 November 2026, 04:16 UTC, before fetching or inspecting empirical rows; Amendment~1 adding the weather protocol at 05:51 UTC before running `analysis.py`; Amendment~2 at 07:32 UTC) tested two host-side premises across open multi-year abundance series ($N \ge 15$ positive steps, $\ge 50\%$ positive fraction, $\ge 75\%$ grid coverage, and $\ge 12$ adjacent step pairs) for bird, fish, small-mammal, freshwater-invertebrate, insect-vector, and tick taxa. Test~T1 required $M \ge 25$ series in $K \ge 5$ independent study/source clusters, a study-clustered $95\%$ bootstrap confidence interval ($1{,}000$ cluster resamples) strictly above zero for mean excess lag-one residual correlation $\Delta r_1 = \hat{r}_1 - \mu_0(\mathbf{t})$ above the exact ordinary-least-squares (OLS) linear-detrending white-noise expectation $\mu_0(\mathbf{t})$, and median bias-calibrated $\mathrm{AR}(1)$ memory $\hat{r}_{1,\mathrm{bc}} > 0$. Test~T2 evaluated illustrative two-stage life cycles with threshold stays $(1, 24)$, $(1, 7.5)$, and $(1, 2.3)$ and required $95\%$ cluster lower bounds above $20\%$ for both the long-cycle $10\%$ small-noise failure fraction ($|\Gamma_0 - D| > 0.10|D|$ or opposite sign) and the short-stage memory-harm zone ($\hat{r}_{1,\mathrm{bc}} > 0.1919$). Pre-analysis synthetic checks (`K0a`--`K0e`, `E0a`--`E0d`) showed that naive uncorrected $\hat{r}_1 < 0$ tests falsely declare negative memory in $91.2\%$--$100.0\%$ of white-noise datasets at $M = 100$--$300$ ($\mu_0 = -1/7$ at $N = 15$), whereas subtracting $\mu_0(\mathbf{t})$ holds two-sided Type~I error at $0.040$--$0.066$.

\paragraph{Data provenance.}
All open archives were fetched on 15 November 2026 via anonymous scripts (`$190.62\ \mathrm{MB}$`): BioTIME (`BioTIMEQuery\_24\_06\_2021.zip`, \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5026943}, plus BioTIME 2.0 metadata v4, \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15222193}, CC BY 4.0, $3{,}032{,}329$ assemblage rows) \citep{biotime2018,dornelas2018,biotime2025,dornelas2025}, the Global Population Dynamics Database v2 (KNB \url{https://doi.org/10.5063/F1BZ63Z8}, CC BY 4.0, $35{,}318$ rows) \citep{prendergast2010}, and VectorByte VecDyn (\url{https://doi.org/10.7274/10083626}, \url{https://vectorbyte.crc.nd.edu/portal/api/}, CC BY-NC 4.0 default or per-dataset CC0/CC BY, $28$ public datasets with $26{,}173$ rows, excluding synthetically downscaled `VectAbundance` series beforehand) \citep{rund2023,neon2023mosquito}. The Living Planet Database (\url{https://www.livingplanetindex.org/data_portal}, inspected 15 November 2026) required manual personal-detail registration and barred redistribution, so $0$ rows were downloaded from it. After applying the `fix1` BioTIME seasonal-cutoff correction (`med\_mo >= 2.0`), the primary sample comprised $1{,}781$ eligible series across $184$ clusters ($884$ GPDD, $887$ BioTIME, $10$ VecDyn; $1{,}542$ annual, $191$ monthly, $48$ seasonal; $903$ birds, $524$ fish, $261$ small mammals, $83$ freshwater invertebrates, $7$ ticks, and $3$ vector insects; strict CC-BY-only BioTIME+GPDD subset $1{,}771/1{,}781$ series in $181/184$ clusters).

\paragraph{Confounders and handling.}
Linear trend bias in $\hat{r}_1$ was removed analytically via $\mu_0(\mathbf{t})$ and finite-sample $\mathrm{AR}(1)$ quadratic-form inversion. Uncorrelated log-observation error, which inflates $\hat{\sigma}^2$ and attenuates $|\hat{r}_1|$, was bounded in `S8b` ($25\%$ observation-variance subtraction, memory-harm fraction $48.4\%$ pre-`fix1`, T1/T2 still supporting). Cross-plot BioTIME pooling was tested in `S6` by switching to unmanipulated fixed-plot `(STUDY\_ID, PLOT, species)` series with GPDD reliability $\ge 3$ ($20{,}177$ series in $80$ clusters, $\Delta r_1 = 0.1861$, memory-harm share $51.3\%$, T1/T2 supporting). Non-$\mathrm{AR}(1)$ lags (`S4` lag-1--10 lineage sums), encounter elasticity $\kappa = 1/2$ (`S9a`), and fine-step contact-limited waiting mortality ($d = 0.125$ on bounded crash host `low` $= 0.05$ across $1{,}326$ in-grid series, `S9b`, lowering long-stage buffering $D \le \sigma^2/4$ from $99.9\%$ to $60.7\%$) bounded life-history sensitivities. Because none of the three compilations records infection prevalence or intensity, causal parasite $R_0$ loss could not be tested.

\paragraph{Weather and environment.}
Open climate archives matched $1{,}681/1{,}781$ corrected primary series across $169/184$ clusters in `fix1` ($1{,}066$ TerraClimate V1.1 monthly $1/24^\circ$ land grids, $386$ NOAA ERSSTv5 monthly $2^\circ$ ocean grids, $119$ NOAA GHCNm v4 monthly stations, and $110$ Daymet V4R1 daily $1\ \mathrm{km}$ grids; $100/1{,}781$ unmatched due to $< 15$ current-lag pairs [$37$], $< 15$ matched steps [$34$], no ocean grid within $250\ \mathrm{km}$ [$22$, including $20$ inland GPDD records misclassified as `Marine` by habitat string], $< 12$ residual pairs [$2$], missing coordinates [$4$], and calendar-date limits [$1$], all accessed 15 November 2026). Regressing log host abundance on linear trend, current and physical lag-one temperature, and complete contemporaneous precipitation (`W1`) using projection-specific white-noise expectations gave adjusted $\Delta r_1 = 0.2876$ ($[0.2503, 0.3345]$, median $\hat{r}_{1,\mathrm{bc}} = 0.3523$), leaving overall T1 and T2 **supports**. Illustrative $Q_{10}$ development-rate sensitivity (`W3`, Zuo et al.\ 2012, partial reading recorded) confirmed sensitivity to thermal clocks without calibrating a specific parasite.

\paragraph{Registered results.}
In the `fix1` corrected primary sample ($1{,}781$ series, $184$ clusters), overall T1 **supports** positive host memory ($\Delta r_1 = 0.3475$ $[0.3076, 0.3905]$, median $\hat{r}_{1,\mathrm{bc}} = 0.3885$; CC-BY-only $1{,}771/181$: $0.3469$ $[0.3056, 0.3924]$). Broad taxon groups meeting $M \ge 25, K \ge 5$ **support** (`birds` $903/92$, $\Delta r_1 = 0.3271$; `fish` $524/30$, $0.3471$; `small\_mammals` $261/59$, $0.3908$; `freshwater\_invertebrates` $83/9$, $0.4222$), whereas `insects\_vectors` ($3/2$), `arachnid\_vectors\_ticks` ($7/1$), and `freshwater\_invertebrates:annual` ($43/3$) are **not decided** ($K < 5$). Under `fix1`, the non-rodent/non-other subgroup `taxon\_subgroup:small\_mammals` dropped from $50/5$ to $34/4$ series/clusters, changing its registered T1 verdict from `supports` to **not decided** in both primary ($\Delta r_1 = 0.2794$ $[0.1948, 0.4564]$) and weather-adjusted ($34/4$) analyses because $K = 4 < 5$. Overall T2 **supports** departure from the memoryless small-noise regime: $59.0\%$ ($1{,}051/1{,}781$, $[48.0\%, 72.6\%]$) of series exceed $10\%$ small-noise error on $(1, 24)$, and $70.3\%$ ($1{,}252/1{,}781$, $[66.3\%, 74.8\%]$) lie in the short-stage memory-harm zone ($\hat{r}_{1,\mathrm{bc}} > 0.1919$). Majority buffering ($D \le \sigma^2/4$) held in $99.9\%$, $99.7\%$, and $44.2\%$ of pre-`fix1` passive geometric series on $(1, 24)$, $(1, 7.5)$, and $(1, 2.3)$ ($99.9\%$, $99.7\%$, and $50.1\%$--$50.4\%$ weather-adjusted).

\paragraph{Independent replication and corrections.}
An independent C and Python re-implementation reproduced all eligibility counts, estimators, and stochastic regime fractions (**verdict: reproduced**) and identified two decision-class implementation discrepancies, both repaired in `fix1` on 15 November 2026: (i) the BioTIME loader had used `med\_mo >= 2.5` rather than the registered `2.0` cutoff for seasonal 2--4-month studies, moving $30$ studies ($61$ previously annual series in `BT\_STUDY\_78`, `333`, `366`, $0$ newly eligible) and changing `taxon\_subgroup:small\_mammals` ($34/4$) to **not decided**; and (ii) `W1` had queried lag-one weather only at positive-host steps rather than physical calendar steps (recovering $1{,}681/1{,}781$ matched series in `fix1` while enforcing $\ge 15$ current GHCN windows). A separate arithmetic correction revised the `S9b` positivity prose bound from $\sigma < 1.387$ to $\sigma < \sqrt{\ln 6.70} = 1.3792$ (outside the $\sigma \le 1.32$ executed grid, $0$ nodes changed), and noted an unfinished post-report weather expansion (`Amendment 3`, $1{,}127$ manifest files) whose unexecuted quadratic/vapor-pressure-deficit models are not claimed here.
