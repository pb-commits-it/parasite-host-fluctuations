## Grade definitions and interpretation

Quoted from COVERAGE.md (internal review record; not distributed), line 7:

> **full** means the entire manuscript statement is checked with only textbook or model assumptions. **core** means the explicitly described component meets that standard and its own reasoning is checked; listed omissions remain outside coverage. **partial-P1/P2/P3** means the credited component retains an owed step, using the weakest class it needs. P1 has a paper proof checked by a second party; P2 has a paper proof without a matching second-party check; P3 has no located proof.

“Full” therefore does **not** mean that Lean derives every textbook probability theorem from its foundations. For example, `prop:model-loss` explicitly assumes a named Furstenberg–Kesten expected-growth limit; `prop:mech-fixed` uses cycle-shift averages and the Gaussian log mean; `prop:exact-P2` uses a textbook reflection identity. Such theorem hypotheses are not new Lean `axiom` declarations and do not appear in `#print axioms`. A clean axiom report alone cannot establish manuscript fidelity.

“Partial-P1” is partial coverage with remaining paper-specific hypotheses, **not zero coverage**. All 82 rows have checked credited declarations. The lower of conflicting grades is retained unless a later dated fidelity report explicitly supports an upgrade. No new upgrade is inferred merely from a file name, successful compilation, or existence of an uncredited helper.
